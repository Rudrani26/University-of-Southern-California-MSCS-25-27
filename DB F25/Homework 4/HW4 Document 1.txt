Title: Chicken Tikka Overview

Chicken tikka is a popular South Asian dish made from boneless pieces of chicken marinated in yogurt and spices. Common spices in the marinade include turmeric, cumin, coriander, chili powder, garlic, and ginger.

The marinated chicken is traditionally cooked in a tandoor, which gives it a smoky flavor and slightly charred edges. Modern versions can also be cooked on a grill or in an oven.

Chicken tikka is often served with lemon wedges, onions, and mint chutney. It can also be used in wraps, salads, or cooked further with a creamy sauce to make chicken tikka masala.

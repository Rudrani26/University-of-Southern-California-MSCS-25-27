Title: Mutton Curry Basics

Mutton curry is a slow-cooked dish made using goat or lamb meat, simmered with a mixture of onions, tomatoes, yogurt, and spices. Popular spices include garam masala, turmeric, cinnamon, cloves, and bay leaves.

The key to a tender mutton curry is long cooking on low heat, allowing the connective tissues to break down. Pressure cookers are also commonly used to speed up this process.

Different regions have unique variations—some versions use coconut milk, others rely on roasted spices, and some include potatoes. Mutton curry is typically served with rice, chapati, or naan.
